Got Questions?

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before making the switch to solar.

What is the lifespan of a solar panel?

Our Tier-1 Mono-PERC panels come with a 25-year linear performance warranty and can last 30+ years with minimal degradation. They retain over 80% efficiency even after 25 years.

How much government subsidy can I get?

Under the PM Surya Ghar scheme, you can get up to 40% central subsidy for residential systems up to 3KW. We handle all documentation and subsidy claims for you at no extra cost.

Does solar work on cloudy or rainy days?

Yes! Modern Mono-PERC panels capture diffused sunlight efficiently. Plus, with net-metering, you stay connected to the grid, so you never run out of power. Excess energy generated is exported to the grid.

What is the payback period for a solar system?

Most residential systems achieve payback within 3-5 years. After that, you enjoy virtually free electricity for the remaining 20+ years of the panel's life. Commercial systems often pay back even faster due to tax benefits.

Is there any maintenance required?

Solar panels require very minimal maintenance — just occasional cleaning to remove dust. We include 5 years of free maintenance with every installation and offer affordable annual maintenance contracts thereafter.
